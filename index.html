Donte's Anime Reviews

This webpage is a list of Anime and what I would rank them. (0-100)

Bleach

A boy with the ability to see spirits finds himself suddenly attacked by one. A strange woman with a sword comes to his aid. Before the final blow is struck she gives him her powers so he can slay the beast.

Rating:
86

Dan-da-Dan

This anime is about a boy and a girl who bet on wheter or not ghosts or aliens exist. The series follow these two characters as they face threats, danger and feelings.

Rating:
50

Heavenly Delusion

Heavenly Delusion is a manga/anime-adapted series based in a post-apocalyptic Tokyo 15 years after disaster struck and ended the world. The story is told from two narrative standpoints (Maru/Kiruko and Tokio). The duo is set on discovering "heaven" while the other is set on venturing "outside".

Rating:
60

Naruto

About a ninja who was born with a demon fox inside him. This demon fox destroyed the village just before he was born so the village he lives in has resented him since he was born.

Rating:
70

My Hero Academia

My Hero Academia is an anime series and manga series about a boy who enrolls in a prestigious hero academy after gaining superpowers from the world's greatest hero

Score:
60
